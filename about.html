About Growlane

Run by Asad Ali.

Three years of hands-on social media, web, and marketing work across retail, home services, and organic goods brands. Currently managing social media for Avaitus Digital across their Australia, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia branches.

The story

From one client to twenty-two brands.

Growlane started with a single retail account and a simple idea: brands don't need a whole in-house team to look and grow like one. What began as posting and replying grew into a full studio — strategy, design, reels, ads, and now web & app development — run by one accountable person and a small vetted team.

Today Growlane manages content and growth for brands across Pakistan, Australia and Saudi Arabia, spanning retail, home services, food, and organic goods.
