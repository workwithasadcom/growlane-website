Your Instagram is working. Is your website?

It's a common gap: the Instagram page looks sharp, the reels are getting views, the DMs are coming in — and then the link in bio leads to a website that still looks like it was built years ago. That mismatch costs trust right at the moment someone was ready to buy.

A visitor who lands on a slow, cluttered, or outdated site reads it as a signal about the business itself, even if the social content said otherwise. First impressions on a website form in seconds, before a single word is read.

What to fix first

Start with load speed and a clear first screen — what the business does, and one obvious next step. Everything else, from colour to copy, should support that one action instead of competing with it.

A website doesn't need to be flashy to convert. It needs to be fast, clear, and consistent with the brand the audience already knows from social.
